Our Services
What We Do

Two core services, one clean workflow

Fiber Laser Cutting

1500W fiber laser cutting mild steel from 11ga up to 7/16". Tight tolerances, clean edges, and intricate detail on a 5×10 bed.

  • Mild steel, hot & cold rolled
  • Up to 60" × 120" sheets
  • Holes, slots, and custom contours

Press Brake Bending

Precision forming on our 60-ton press brake — turning flat cut parts into finished brackets, enclosures, and weldments with consistent, repeatable bends.

  • 60-ton press brake capacity
  • Brackets, flanges & enclosures
  • Repeatable bend angles
  • Pairs with our laser-cut blanks
